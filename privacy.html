Privacy policy

MathYTB uploader. Effective 6 October 2026.

Who uses it

MathYTB uploader is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, who signs in with their own Google account to manage their own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses two YouTube scopes: youtube.upload, to upload videos, and youtube.force-ssl, to set titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails and to read the channel's own videos and playlists. It accesses nothing outside the owner's channel.

What it stores

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

What it does not do

It collects no data about anyone else. It has no analytics or tracking. It does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone.

Google API Services

MathYTB uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.
