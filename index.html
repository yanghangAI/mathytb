MathYTB uploader

A personal tool, used only by its owner.

MathYTB uploader is a small program that its owner runs on their own computer to publish the maths explainer videos they make. Through the YouTube Data API, signed in with the owner's own Google account, it:

  • uploads the owner's videos to the owner's own YouTube channel;
  • sets each video's title, description and thumbnail, and adds its captions;
  • reads the owner's own channel: its videos and playlists.

It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else signs in to it.
