MathYTB

A personal YouTube publishing application, used only by its owner.

Purpose

MathYTB is an application that publishes maths explainer videos to its owner's YouTube channel. The owner makes the videos on their own computer; MathYTB then uses the YouTube Data API, signed in with the owner's own Google account, to:

  • upload each finished video to the owner's own channel, as a private video;
  • set the video's title, description and thumbnail;
  • add the video's captions;
  • read the owner's own channel (its videos and playlists) to keep track of what has been published.

MathYTB is not offered to anyone else. No one other than the owner signs in to it, and it never accesses anyone else's account or channel.
